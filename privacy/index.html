Jedi Co-op privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Jedi Co-op is a free, fan-made online co-op mod for STAR WARS Jedi: Fallen Order and STAR WARS Jedi: Survivor. This page says what it collects, why, how long it's kept, and how to have it removed. The short version: as little as possible, never sold or shared, and deleted when you ask.

Signing in with Discord

While Jedi Co-op is in closed testing, the launcher asks you to sign in with Discord so we can see whether you've been approved to play. Signing in happens on Discord's own page; we never see your password.

  • What we get: your Discord user ID and your Discord name. Not your email, friends, servers or messages.
  • What it's for: checking whether you have the Approved role in the Jedi Co-op Discord server, using the Jedi Co-op bot that's already in that server. Nothing else.
  • Where it's kept: on your own PC, in a sign-in file the launcher and the mod read, for up to 90 days or until you sign out. Our server doesn't keep a list of players. When you sign out, it remembers that sign-in as cancelled until it would have expired.
  • No lasting access: Jedi Co-op doesn't keep access to your Discord account. The permission Discord gives us is cancelled straight after you sign in.

Your Discord profile

While you play, the mod tells the Discord app on your PC what you're doing (in the menus, playing solo, hosting a co-op journey or playing with a friend), and Discord shows it on your profile. This goes straight to the Discord app on your PC, not through us. Your join code is only shared with a friend you accept in Discord. You can turn it off with Presence=0 under [Discord] in JediCoop.ini.

Playing co-op

  • To connect you with your friend, the Jedi Co-op server sees the player name you chose, your join code and your internet address, only while you're connecting and playing.
  • A co-op journey's save is kept on the server so your friend can join it, and is deleted 90 days after the host last played it, or straight away when the host deletes the journey.
  • Bug reports and feedback you send from the launcher are kept to fix problems, with what the launcher shows you before you send.
  • The server's logs (for example "a player signed in") are kept for up to 30 days.

What we never do

We don't sell or share anything about you, show ads, or build profiles of players. Nothing is used for anything but running Jedi Co-op.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in the launcher: it removes the sign-in from your PC and cancels it on our server.
  • Ask us to delete anything else we hold about you: post in the Jedi Co-op Discord or message the staff there, and we'll delete it.

Age

Jedi Co-op's sign-in is for people aged 13 or over, the same as Discord.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will say so, with a new date at the top.
